# Comparison / "X vs Y" article template

Use for decision-stage articles where the reader is choosing between two options
(e.g. "IHM ou SCADA", "Réparer ou remplacer un variateur", "TIA Portal ou Step 7").

1. **H1** — "X ou Y : lequel choisir (pour …)".
2. **Intro** — the real decision the reader faces, in 2–3 lines.
3. **The short answer** — a one-paragraph verdict (often: "it depends on …, and sometimes you need both").
4. **What X is** (H2) — plain definition + when it fits. Concrete, honest.
5. **What Y is** (H2) — same.
6. **Which to choose** (H2) — a short decision list by situation:
   - Situation A → X
   - Situation B → Y
   - Situation C → both
   *(A small table is fine, but keep it readable on a phone.)*
7. **The catch** (H2, optional) — the thing people get wrong (networks, cost, downtime…).
8. **How MTE helps** — one honest paragraph; MTE does **both** sides when true (never push one to sell).
9. **CTA** — WhatsApp + devis.
10. **FAQ** — 2–3 real objection-style questions.

Rules:
- Be genuinely balanced — don't strawman the option you'd rather sell.
- Only claim capabilities in `features.yaml`.
- Link to the pillar(s) for both options + one `/outils` tool when relevant.
