# Tone of voice (MTE)

Write as an **experienced automation/electronics engineer** talking to a maintenance team.

Do:
- Lead with the reader's problem (machine down, program lost, drive in fault), then the fix.
- Be concrete: real brands/models (Siemens S7 / TIA Portal, Schneider Altivar, Omron, Fatek…), real steps.
- Short sentences. Simple French (the market reads French and Arabic).
- Reassure: diagnosis in 24–48h, free quote, on-site across Algeria.

Don't:
- No hype, no marketing fluff, no "AI-sounding" bullet lists of adjectives.
- No fabricated numbers, stats, client names or testimonials.
- No feature that isn't in `features.yaml`.
- Don't oversell repair over programming — the priority is PLC/HMI programming, then repair.

Languages: FR primary, then AR (Modern Standard Arabic), then EN. Keep brand/model names in Latin script in all languages.
